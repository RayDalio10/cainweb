Our Journey of Impact

What have we done with your help?

We’ve invested over $240,000 to bring more than 13 transformative projects to life, impacting over 14,285 underprivileged people across seven under-resourced African communities.

In Lagos's largest community, we established a state-of-the-art educational resource center. In Jeida, a rural village near Abuja, we collaborated to establish the first school, creating new opportunities for children to thrive.

"Purpose, passion, and persistence can change lives. We are dismantling barriers to opportunity."

May Asagba Founder & CEO
