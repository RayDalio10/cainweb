Who We Are

About CAIN Foundation

CAIN (Catering to Africans In Need) Educational Foundation is a non-profit organisation registered in both the United States and Nigeria, based in Ikotun, Lagos.

We are driven by a single mission: to empower underprivileged African children and youth with access to quality education and essential digital skills.

Through a systems-based, community-driven approach, we work directly with underrepresented communities to close the digital divide.
