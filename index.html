Our Story

Reimagining Education Since 2013

Founded by May Oghenerukeme Asagba at just 21, CAIN was born from a vow that no child's future should be dictated by poverty.

What began as a dream in a university dorm room is now a movement. Today, we operate a tech-enabled learning community in Ikotun, Lagos, equipped with a modern library, high-speed internet, and mentorship programs that are rewriting destinies.

"Purpose, passion, and persistence can change lives. We are dismantling barriers to opportunity for Africa's next generation." — May Asagba, Founder & CEO
