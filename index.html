Our Story

Reimagining Education Since 2013

Founded by May Oghenerukeme Asagba at just 21, CAIN was born from a deep-seated belief in educational reform.

Today, we operate a state-of-the-art educational resource center in Ikotun, Lagos, providing students with digital skills, mentorship, and career opportunities that once seemed impossible.

"Purpose, passion, and persistence can change lives. We are dismantling barriers to opportunity." — May Asagba, Founder & CEO
